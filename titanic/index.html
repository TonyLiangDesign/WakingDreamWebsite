Back to Titanic · Project in development

History, felt as a place.

An immersive project in development, shaped around the experience of being aboard Titanic. Through its spaces, light and atmosphere, we are exploring a more personal encounter with the past.

Follow the project The experience

The experience

Space. Atmosphere. Presence.

A view across the open deck. The change of light as you step inside. The scale of a staircase, understood by standing at its foot. These are the moments we want to make possible.

Our aim is to make history tangible through the act of exploring, with room for curiosity, quiet observation and reflection.

Exploration

A sense of being there.

The experience is being designed around movement and observation: stepping onto a deck, following a passage, pausing in a room. Each space is part of a larger journey, with time to notice the details along the way.

Reconstruction

Built with care for the past.

The reconstruction takes shape from the ship's proportions to its rooms and fittings. Historical research guides the work, while spatial detail gives it a human scale. The images shown here are from an evolving prototype; spaces and details remain in development.

Work in progress

A world taking shape.

Early views of the reconstruction, from the ship's outline to the spaces within. The project is not yet open to the public; a release date has not been announced.
