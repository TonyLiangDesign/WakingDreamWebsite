OneChair · Private beta

A film studio in one chair.

Write the story, stage every shot, generate the frames, and keep the whole film on one board. OneChair is for directors who have the film but not the crew.

Visit onechair.ai How it works

The board

One board holds the whole film.

Every scene, shot, character and look is a card on a single canvas. Cards connect to the cards they came from, so when a character changes, every shot that uses them knows. Nothing lives in a folder you have to remember.

BOARD · SCREENSHOT

The story room

A screenplay that stays structured, and a first AD who reads it.

Adapt a novel or write from a blank page. The script is kept scene by scene, line by line, so you can edit one beat without breaking the rest. A first assistant director tracks what the plot still owes and won't let a scene land until it pays.

STORY ROOM · SCREENSHOT

Previz

Stage the shot before you spend a frame on it.

Put mannequins and a camera in a real 3D set, describe the framing in plain words, and see the shot. Move the camera by talking to it. When the frame is right, that is what gets generated.

PREVIZ · SCREENSHOT

Generation

Pick the model for the job. Pay for the frames you keep.

Text, image, video and voice models from several providers, chosen per card. Billing is by credit, and no provider keys ever touch your device.
