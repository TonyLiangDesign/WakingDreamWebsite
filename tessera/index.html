Tessera · 3D creation for iOS & Mac

Describe a scene. Watch it render.

A 3D creation app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Begin with a description, then refine the objects, materials and light until the scene feels yours. Coming soon.

Get notified at launch How it works

From words to a scene

Begin with words. Shape the details.

Describe what you have in mind. AI helps generate and arrange the scene, while you guide the composition, adjust materials and find the right light. The scene remains editable as your idea evolves.

Generated on your device

More room to explore.

On supported hardware, 3D model generation runs on your device. Explore variations, rework a prop and follow an unexpected direction. Device requirements will be shared before launch.

Light and material

See the scene come into focus.

Interactive path tracing reveals how light moves through the scene. Reflections, shadows and material detail resolve as you refine the image, with MetalFX denoising helping it become clear.
