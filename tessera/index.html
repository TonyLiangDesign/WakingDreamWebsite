Tessera · Coming soon for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Describe a scene. Watch it render.

Tessera turns ideas into 3D scenes: describe what you want, and AI generates and arranges objects, materials, and lighting, while you refine every detail. No traditional 3D workflow required.

Get notified at launch How it works

Describe, don't model

Say what you want. The scene assembles itself.

Type a sentence and Tessera generates the objects, places them, dresses them in materials and sets the light. Then it hands you the scene: every object, every material, every lamp stays yours to move, swap or tune, in words or by hand.

Generated on your device

Experiment freely. Nothing leaves the room.

On supported devices, models generate entirely on device, so you can experiment freely without cloud fees. Try ten versions of a prop, throw nine away, and pay nothing for the ones you didn't keep.

ON-DEVICE GENERATION · RECORDING

Rendered like a film frame

Path traced, denoised, and finished while you watch.

Interactive path tracing and MetalFX denoising turn your scene into a richly detailed, high-quality render. Light bounces where it should, glass refracts, skin scatters, and the image resolves in front of you instead of in a queue.
