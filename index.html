About

One studio, three ways of making a picture real.

Waking Dream Technology is a small studio founded by Tony Liang. Everything we build starts from the same question: how far can a person with a clear picture in their head get without a crew, a render farm, or years of training?

OneChair answers it for film. Tessera answers it for 3D. Back to Titanic is the question asked of a real place and a real night.
