About the studio

A small studio for large pictures.

Waking Dream Technology was founded in Auckland, New Zealand by Tony Liang, a designer who kept running into the same wall: the picture was clear, and everything between the picture and the finished thing was not.

What we make

Three projects, one question.

OneChair is a film studio in one chair: story, previz, generation and the whole film on one board, for directors without a crew.

Tessera is 3D without the 3D workflow: describe a scene, and it is generated, lit and path traced on your own device.

Back to Titanic is the same idea turned on a real place: a full-ship reconstruction of RMS Titanic you can walk, and the five days of April 1912 told from inside it.

We are a small team and we ship in the open. Each project page carries its current status honestly, including what is not built yet.
