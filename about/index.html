About the studio

Giving imagination a sense of place.

Founded by Tony Liang in Auckland, New Zealand, Waking Dream Technology works between image and experience, giving ideas a form we can see, shape and inhabit.

What we make

Image. Imagination. Presence.

Unreal Visualisation explores how materials, light and motion can make a virtual world feel real, from automotive details to digital characters.

Tessera is a 3D creation app in development for iOS, iPadOS and macOS. It brings AI-assisted scene creation and rendering to iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Back to Titanic is an immersive project in development. Its focus is the experience of being aboard: the scale of the ship, the atmosphere of its spaces and the history they hold.

Across these projects, the interest is the same: how a digital image becomes something we feel part of.
